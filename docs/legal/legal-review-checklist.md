# Legal review checklist

This is a practical handoff for qualified privacy and technology counsel. It is not legal
advice. The current Privacy, Terms, DPA, and Subprocessor pages were drafted with AI assistance
from the implemented product and architecture. They have not been reviewed or approved by a
lawyer and must remain marked as drafts.

SecondTrip is limited to fictional or properly anonymized imported datasets until counsel
approves final documentation and the product implements every promise made in that text.

## Operator details to provide

- [ ] Legal name or registered entity name
- [ ] Business address
- [ ] Country and governing jurisdiction
- [ ] Support contact name and address
- [ ] Privacy contact name and address
- [ ] Tax or business registration details, if applicable

## Privacy decisions to confirm

- [ ] Controller and processor roles for account, contact, imported, derived, audit, and support
      data
- [ ] Lawful basis for each processing purpose
- [ ] Retention periods that are actually enforced by the product and providers
- [ ] Procedures and ownership for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and
      portability requests
- [ ] Incident and breach assessment, escalation, documentation, and notification process
- [ ] Whether a Data Protection Officer, National Privacy Commission registration, or other
      Philippines-specific requirement applies
- [ ] Exact subprocessor legal entities, processing purposes, regions, and support-access paths
- [ ] International-transfer locations and mechanisms
- [ ] Whether SecondTrip targets or accepts users in the EU, UK, US, or other jurisdictions with
      additional requirements

## Terms decisions to confirm

- [ ] User eligibility and authority to act for an organization
- [ ] Private-beta availability, support, change, suspension, and closure expectations
- [ ] Acceptable-use restrictions, including the current fictional/anonymized-data-only rule
- [ ] Ownership and licensing of customer data, product software, documentation, and feedback
- [ ] Confidentiality obligations
- [ ] Warranties and disclaimers
- [ ] Liability scope and any cap
- [ ] Indemnity
- [ ] Termination and post-termination data handling
- [ ] Governing law, venue, and dispute process
- [ ] Notice for policy changes
- [ ] Electronic acceptance, policy versioning, and evidence retained for each acceptance

## DPA decisions to confirm

- [ ] Documented processing instructions and subject matter
- [ ] Personnel confidentiality obligations
- [ ] Technical and organizational security measures
- [ ] Subprocessor appointment and change process
- [ ] Assistance with data-subject requests and compliance assessments
- [ ] Incident and breach notification commitments
- [ ] Data deletion or return at the end of service
- [ ] Audit and information rights
- [ ] International-transfer terms
- [ ] Required processing, security, and subprocessor annexes

## Operational launch gate

Do not remove the draft notices, `noindex, nofollow` metadata, or fictional/anonymized-data-only
restriction until qualified counsel approves the final text and the product implements and tests
every promise in it. Record the approved versions, publication date, acceptance mechanism, and
release SHA before allowing real customer production data.
